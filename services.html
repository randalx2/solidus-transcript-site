How to request a job

Tell us: (1) approximate audio duration or page count, (2) number of speakers and any overlap or background noise, (3) input formats, (4) required HTML, PDF, transcript or other outputs, and (5) your deadline. Send only a description initially, not confidential source evidence. We confirm feasibility, privacy/retention terms, written quote and payment method before accepting the files.
